Built for Fleet Carrier Trading

Trade Finder

Search locally cached market data for loading and delivery opportunities with stock, demand, pad size, pricing and profit information.

Carrier Pricing

Calculate carrier BUY and SELL settings around Galactic Average pricing while accounting for loader and unloader profit per tonne.

Carrier Operations

Optional Frontier CAPI integration is designed to show authorized fleet-carrier status, commodity orders and loading or unloading progress.

EDDN + Local Data

A local market database can be bootstrapped from public galaxy data and refreshed with newer EDDN market messages while the desktop application is running.

Frontier Account Authorization

Fleet Carrier Trade Command uses Frontier's official OAuth authorization flow for features that require private Commander or Fleet Carrier data. The application does not ask users to enter their Frontier password into Fleet Carrier Trade Command. Authentication takes place on Frontier's own website, and access can be revoked through Frontier's authorized-applications controls.

Carrier data is treated as a snapshot rather than a real-time transaction feed. The application therefore refreshes carrier information periodically instead of continuously polling Frontier services.
